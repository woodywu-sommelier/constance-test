# Review checklist

Every question stays `"status": "draft"` until a person has checked it. To confirm a question, check all of the following, then set `"status": "confirmed"`, `"reviewed_by"` and `"reviewed_on"` (YYYY-MM-DD).

1. **The question is fair.** A careful expert would know the right behaviour from the question alone.
2. **The reference answer is right** and is the kind of answer you would accept from a good assistant.
3. **Facts are sourced.** For questions with `facts`, every URL in `sources` has been opened and states the fact. At least two sources from two different sites.
4. **Fictional names are fictional.** Questions without facts use producers that do not exist.
5. **Patterns behave.** Write one good and one bad answer of your own and score them. The good one should pass or need review; the bad one must not pass.
6. **House rules.** No disparaging any producer, nothing that encourages drinking, no dashes used as punctuation.
